# Field sampling principles

## Quick Diagnosis is not Field Survey

A Quick Diagnosis describes only the photographed sample and the limits of that image. It must not claim whole-field prevalence, incidence, severity, or risk.

A Field Survey requires multiple geographically distributed samples collected using a crop/problem-specific protocol. Field-level estimates must preserve the sample locations, sampling method, denominator, timing, exclusions, uncertainty, and applicable agronomic evidence.

One photograph can support an image-level observation. It can never justify a fabricated field percentage. Disease severity within a photographed leaf or plant is also distinct from field incidence across sampled units.
